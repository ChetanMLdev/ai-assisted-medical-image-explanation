# Requirements Document

## Overview
This document outlines the high level functional and non functional requirements for the AI assisted medical image explanation system. The requirements are intended to guide system design and implementation.

## Functional Requirements
- The system shall allow users to upload medical images such as X rays, CT scans, and MRIs.
- The system shall analyze images to identify relevant regions and visual patterns.
- The system shall generate non diagnostic, plain language explanations of observable features.
- The system shall display confidence levels for generated explanations.
- The system shall support language aligned explanations in English and native languages.
- The system shall provide visual highlights for regions of interest.

## Non Functional Requirements
- The system shall use only public or synthetic datasets.
- The system shall avoid disease identification or treatment recommendations.
- The system shall ensure explainability and transparency.
- The system shall be designed for time efficient healthcare workflows.
- The system shall be scalable and modular.

## Constraints
- The system is intended for educational and assistive use only.
- Clinical verification is required before any medical decision making.
